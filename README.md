## 📁 Featured Projects

### 1. [Income and Life Expectancy, 1952–2007](https://github.com/ismaeladan38-art/life-expectancy-analysis)
**Question:** How strongly is income related to life expectancy, and is Africa's gap with the rest of the world closing?
**Data:** Gapminder, 142 countries × 12 years (1,704 rows)
**Tools:** Python (pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, seaborn), regression and residual analysis
**Key finding:** Income explains about 65% of the variation in life expectancy (r = 0.81), yet Africa's gap with the rest of the world widened from 15.7 years in 1952 to 19.3 years in 2007.
